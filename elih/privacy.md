Namaz vakti bildirimleri ve ezan sesi sadece iznin varsa, telefonunun kendisinde planlanır; hiçbir sunucuya gönderilmez.

Uygunsuz reklam kategorileri (kumar, alkol, flört, cinsellik, siyaset, başka dinler) engellenmiştir. Ayrıntılar: [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

Für die Gebetszeiten kannst du deine Stadt aus einer Liste wählen oder mit deiner Erlaubnis deinen ungefähren Standort verwenden. ## English

### 1. Summary

Elih is an informational app with prayers, Quran verses, prayer times and Kurdish language, history and culture. No account is needed. We do not collect your name, email, contacts or photos. The app shows Google AdMob ads.

### 2. Location and prayer times

For prayer times you can pick your city from a list or, with your permission, use your approximate location. Times are calculated on your device; your location is never sent to us or any other server. You can revoke permission in your phone's Settings at any time.

### 3. Notifications and adhan

Prayer-time notifications and the adhan are scheduled on your phone only with your permission; nothing is sent to a server.

### 4. Settings and favorites

Your language, favorite prayers and settings are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

### 5. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through AdMob by Google Ireland Ltd. Google may process your advertising ID (IDFA, only with your permission), IP address and usage data. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first; you can change it in the app under "Ad privacy settings". Unsuitable ad categories (gambling, alcohol, dating, sexual content, politics, other religions) are blocked. Details: [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### 6. Audio and external links

Recitations and prayers may be loaded from the internet; the provider of the audio file receives your device's IP address. The link to elih-verein.de opens in your browser, where that site's privacy rules apply.

### 7. Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
