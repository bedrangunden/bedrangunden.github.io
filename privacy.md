Google bunun için cihaz reklam kimliği (IDFA, sadece izin verirsen), IP adresinden çıkan yaklaşık konum, reklam etkileşimleri ve hata/performans bilgilerini işleyebilir. Ayrıntılar: [Google'ın veri kullanımı](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). "Reklamsız oyun" satın alınırsa ara reklamlar gösterilmez; ödüllü reklamlar sadece sen istersen açılır.

**İzinler:** Konum, kişiler, kamera, mikrofon gibi izinler istenmez.

**Satın almalar:** Uygulama içi satın almalar tamamen Apple tarafından işlenir. Ödeme bilgilerini biz görmeyiz. Oyun parası sadece oyunda kullanılır; gerçek paraya çevrilemez.

**Çocuklar:** Uygulama çocuklardan bilerek veri toplamaz.

Im EWR, im Vereinigten Königreich und in der Schweiz wirst du vorher um Einwilligung gebeten (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. a DSGVO); du kannst sie im Spiel unter *Datenschutz & Bedingungen → Werbe-Datenschutzeinstellungen* jederzeit ändern. Mit „Werbefrei spielen“ wird keine Zwischendurch-Werbung mehr gezeigt; Belohnungs-Werbung startet nur, wenn du sie selbst auswählst.

**Berechtigungen:** Es werden keine Berechtigungen wie Standort, Kontakte, Kamera oder Mikrofon angefragt.

**Käufe:** In-App-Käufe werden vollständig von Apple abgewickelt. Wir sehen keine Zahlungsdaten. Spielgeld ist nur im Spiel nutzbar und nicht in echtes Geld umtauschbar.

**Schriftarten:** Die App kann Schriftarten von Google Fonts laden. Dabei wird die IP-Adresse deines Geräts an Google übermittelt; wir erhalten diese nicht.

## English

**In short:** We (the developer of Galerici) do not collect personal data. The game shows ads from Google AdMob; Google processes some data for these ads (see below).

**Save data:** Your progress (cash, cars, level, settings) is stored only on your device and is not sent to us or any other server. Deleting the app deletes your save.

**Ads (Google AdMob):** The app shows ads through AdMob by Google Ireland Ltd. For this, Google may process your device's advertising ID (IDFA, only with your permission), approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions and crash/performance data. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first; you can change it in the game under *Privacy & terms → Ad privacy settings*. You can turn off tracking under *Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking*. Details: [How Google uses data](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Buying the ad-free game stops ads between play; reward ads only play when you choose them.

**Permissions:** The app does not request permissions such as location, contacts, camera or microphone.

**Purchases:** In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Game money can only be used in the game and cannot be exchanged for real money.

**Fonts:** The app may load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your device's IP address reaches Google; we do not receive it.
